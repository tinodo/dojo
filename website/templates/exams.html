Add another exam

In Dojo, type an exam code. Dojo reads the official study guide, shows what building the course would cost at list price, and builds it when you agree. A page grounds a skill only when two model families each judge that it teaches the skill and each quote a sentence that Dojo finds on the page word for word.

A skill without a page

While Dojo has no verified official page for a skill, it keeps looking in the background, and does not teach or ask about that skill.
